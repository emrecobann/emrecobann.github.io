I'm an AI engineer at HAVELSAN and an M.Sc. student at Hacettepe University, where I work with Prof. Erkut Erdem on medical large language models: how they are trained, how they reason, and how to evaluate them.

My research asks whether these models follow the evidence the way a clinician would. I train LLMs to reason through the findings before they write a radiology impression, then refine them with reinforcement learning so their conclusions stay faithful and concise. I also build counterfactual patients to test whether a model's explanation changes when the clinical picture does, or whether only its answer moves.

At HAVELSAN I build LLM systems that run on our own infrastructure, from tool-calling agents to speech recognition.
